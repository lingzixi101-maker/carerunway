Clickable concept prototype for the DAISI Challenge 2026 (problem A3). All values are illustrative, from a toy supply model calibrated near the real national figure of 293 seniors per clinic (2022). Under-served means more seniors per clinic than today's worst-served quarter of areas. Population: HDB Sample Household Survey 2018, town labels corrected. Planned clinics: MOH announcements, 2026; locations approximate. Boundaries: URA Master Plan 2019. Areas without HDB survey data are left blank.
